# Master Disclaimers

> **LEGAL-1 disclaimer:** This is a template for information purposes, not legal advice.
> Have a solicitor review it before use.

## 1. Master LEGAL-1 text

The following text is the master disclaimer for this entire compliance and legal
package. It must appear, verbatim, at or near the top of **every** file in
`agency/compliance/` and `agency/legal/`, and should also be referenced in
`agency/README.md`:

> **"This is a template for information purposes, not legal advice. Have a solicitor
> review it before use."**

## 2. Where it must appear

| Location | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Every file in `agency/compliance/` | Verbatim, near the top of the file, before any substantive content |
| Every file in `agency/legal/` | Verbatim, near the top of the file, before any substantive content |
| `agency/README.md` | A reference to this disclaimer and a statement that the compliance/legal pack is not a substitute for solicitor review |
| Any client-facing deliverable derived from these templates (e.g. a printed privacy notice insert, a signed DPA, a signed service agreement) | Retained through to the final, client-facing version — do not strip the disclaimer when adapting a template for a real client, until a solicitor has formally reviewed and taken ownership of the final wording |

## 3. Scope-of-service limits

This entire compliance/legal pack, and the wider `agency/` launch package it is part of,
is subject to the following limits on scope:

1. **Not legal advice.** Nothing in `agency/compliance/` or `agency/legal/` constitutes
   legal advice to the Agency, to any Client, or to any third party. It is drafted as a
   starting-point template only.
2. **Not a guarantee of compliance.** Using these templates does not guarantee compliance
   with UK GDPR, PECR, the DMCC Act, or any other law — actual compliance depends on how
   the Agency and each Client implement, adapt, and operate the templates and workflows
   in practice.
3. **Law changes.** These templates reflect the law and guidance as researched on the
   date noted in `agency/compliance/legal-research-notes.md`. Law, regulator guidance,
   and third-party (GoHighLevel, Google) terms can and do change. Neither the Agency nor
   any Client should treat this pack as permanently up to date — see the ICO/CMA/GHL
   sourcing gaps flagged in `legal-research-notes.md` for specific points needing
   re-verification.
4. **No solicitor-client relationship created.** Producing, reading, or using this pack
   does not create a solicitor-client relationship between the drafter of these
   templates and the Agency or any Client.
5. **Client responsibility.** The Client remains the data controller and the legally
   responsible party for its own compliance with UK GDPR, PECR, and the DMCC Act,
   notwithstanding the Agency's role as processor and the existence of this pack.
