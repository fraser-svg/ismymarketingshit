## Who must pay

Under the Data Protection (Charges and Information) Regulations 2018 (as amended), most
UK organisations that process personal data electronically must pay the ICO's annual
data protection fee, register on the ICO's public register, and renew annually — unless
a specific exemption applies (e.g. certain not-for-profit or purely paper-based
processing — exemptions are narrow and should not be assumed).

**Both of the following must independently register and pay, if not otherwise exempt:**

1. **[Agency Name]** (the Processor) — because it processes personal data (client
   customer contact details) as part of running its business, even though it acts as a
   processor rather than controller for the Client's data.
2. **[Client Business Name]** (the Controller, each and every client of the Agency) —
   because it is the data controller for its own customers' contact data, including the
   data used for review requests.

Neither party's registration substitutes for the other's — a Processor's ICO
registration does not cover its Controller clients, and vice versa.

## Fee tiers (as researched — verify current figures before paying)

| Tier | Qualifying criteria | Standard fee | Direct Debit fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 (micro)** | Turnover ≤ £632,000 and ≤ 10 staff (or qualifying public-sector/charity criteria) | £52 | £47 |
| **Tier 2 (small/medium)** | Turnover ≤ £36 million and ≤ 250 staff | £78 | £73 |
| **Tier 3 (large)** | Anyone not qualifying for Tier 1 or Tier 2 | £3,763 | — |

Charities and small occupational pension schemes pay the Tier 1 rate regardless of size
or turnover.

**These figures are sourced from cross-corroborated secondary sources, not a direct
fetch of the ICO's own current fee page (see `agency/compliance/legal-research-notes.md`
§3 for the sourcing gap). Fee levels sit in secondary legislation and are reviewed
periodically — they can change without a corresponding change to primary law.**

## Action required

Both the Agency and every Client must, before customer data first enters GoHighLevel:

1. Confirm their qualifying tier using the ICO's own **fee self-assessment tool**:
   https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/fee-self-assessment/ [verify
   this URL is still current — the ICO periodically restructures its site].
2. Register and pay via the ICO's online registration service:
   https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/ [verify current URL].
3. Diarise the annual renewal date — the fee is payable annually, not once.
4. Keep evidence of current registration (the ICO registration number) on file, as this
   may be requested by the other party as part of onboarding due diligence (see
   `agency/ops/onboarding-checklist.md`).
