# Data Processing Agreement (Template) — Article 28(3) UK GDPR

> **LEGAL-1 disclaimer:** This is a template for information purposes, not legal advice.
> Have a solicitor review it before use.

This Data Processing Agreement ("**DPA**") must be signed and in place **before** any
Client customer data is entered into GoHighLevel (GDPR-2). It is designed to be attached
to, or incorporated by reference into, the `service-agreement-template.md`.

---

## Parties

This DPA is entered into between:

- **[Client Business Name]**, of [Client registered address] ("the **Controller**"); and
- **[Agency Name]**, of [Agency registered address] ("the **Processor**"),

together "the **Parties**."

---

## 1. Subject matter and duration

1.1 This DPA governs the Processor's processing of personal data on behalf of the
Controller in connection with the review-request automation services described in the
Service Agreement dated [Date] between the Parties.

1.2 This DPA takes effect on [Date] and continues for the duration of the Service
Agreement, and thereafter for as long as the Processor holds any personal data processed
under it.

## 2. Nature and purpose of processing

2.1 **Nature:** collection, storage, organisation, transmission (by SMS and email), and
deletion of Controller customer contact data within GoHighLevel, in order to send review
requests and record consent/opt-out status on the Controller's instructions.

2.2 **Purpose:** to enable the Controller to send compliant review-request messages to
its own customers and to record the lawful basis, delivery, and opt-out status of each
message.

## 3. Categories of data subjects and data types

3.1 **Data subjects:** the Controller's customers who have transacted with the
Controller.

3.2 **Categories of personal data:** name; mobile telephone number and/or email address;
transaction/job reference and date; consent/opt-out basis and date; message delivery
and opt-out/suppression status; (where applicable) review outcome metadata.

3.3 No special category data (Article 9 UK GDPR) is intended to be processed under this
DPA. [If the Controller's sector may generate special category data through review
content — e.g. health data implied in a review of a medical service — flag to a
solicitor before proceeding.]

## 4. Processor obligations (Article 28(3))

The Processor shall:

(a) process personal data only on the Controller's documented instructions (including
regarding international transfers), unless required to do otherwise by UK law, in which
case the Processor shall inform the Controller of that legal requirement before
processing (unless prohibited from doing so);

(b) ensure persons authorised to process the personal data are subject to a duty of
confidentiality;

(c) implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of
security appropriate to the risk (Article 32), including [encryption in transit and at
rest via GoHighLevel's platform security — Processor to confirm current GHL security
documentation];

(d) not engage a sub-processor without the Controller's prior general or specific
written authorisation — **the Controller hereby gives general authorisation** for the
Processor's use of GoHighLevel/LeadConnector LLC as its primary sub-processor platform,
and any further sub-processors GoHighLevel itself engages as listed at
gohighlevel.com/sub-processors (as updated from time to time, with 30 days' notice per
GoHighLevel's own DPA terms — [Agency to confirm this notice mechanism is still current
before relying on it]);

(e) taking into account the nature of the processing, assist the Controller by
appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as possible, for the
fulfilment of the Controller's obligation to respond to data subject rights requests;

(f) assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with the Article 32–36 obligations
(security, breach notification, DPIAs), taking into account the nature of processing and
information available to the Processor;

(g) at the Controller's choice, delete or return all personal data to the Controller
after the end of the provision of services, and delete existing copies unless UK law
requires storage of the personal data;

(h) make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance
with Article 28 obligations, and allow for and contribute to audits, including
inspections, conducted by the Controller or an auditor mandated by the Controller,
subject to reasonable notice and confidentiality.

## 5. Sub-processors

5.1 The Processor's primary sub-processor for storage, messaging (SMS/email), and CRM
functions is **GoHighLevel / LeadConnector LLC**. GoHighLevel's own published
sub-processor list (gohighlevel.com/sub-processors) additionally includes, among others,
Google Cloud Services and Amazon Web Services (data storage), Twilio and Mailgun
(messaging), and other providers as updated from time to time — see
`agency/compliance/legal-research-notes.md` §4 for the current list as researched, and
verify the live page before relying on it.

5.2 The Processor shall ensure any sub-processor is bound by data protection obligations
equivalent to those in this DPA.

## 6. International transfers (GDPR-6)

6.1 The Controller acknowledges that GoHighLevel stores personal data on infrastructure
located in the **United States** (via its sub-processors Google Cloud and Amazon Web
Services).

6.2 As at the date of this DPA's drafting, two transfer mechanisms are understood to be
in place to lawfully permit this transfer under UK GDPR Article 46:

(a) **UK IDTA / UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses**, incorporated into
GoHighLevel's own Data Processing Agreement; and/or

(b) **The UK Extension to the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework** (the "UK–US Data
Bridge"), which GoHighLevel/LeadConnector self-certify to.

6.3 **The Processor and Controller must verify, before each transfer or at least
annually, that:**
- GoHighLevel's DPF certification remains "Active" on the official register at
  dataprivacyframework.gov (DPF certifications lapse if not renewed annually — this was
  not independently confirmed as "Active" in the research underlying this template; see
  `agency/compliance/legal-research-notes.md` §4); and
- GoHighLevel's current sub-processor list and DPA terms have not materially changed.

6.4 [Solicitor to confirm this clause remains adequate if GoHighLevel's transfer
mechanisms change, or if the Controller/Processor engages any other US-based
sub-processor not covered by GoHighLevel's own terms.]

## 7. Breach notification

7.1 The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event
within [48 hours] of becoming aware, of any personal data breach affecting the
Controller's data, providing sufficient information to allow the Controller to meet any
of its own breach-notification obligations (including, where applicable, to the ICO
within 72 hours of the Controller becoming aware).

7.2 The notification shall include, so far as available: the nature of the breach; the
categories and approximate number of data subjects and records affected; the likely
consequences; and the measures taken or proposed to address the breach.

## 8. Deletion / return of data

8.1 On termination or expiry of the Service Agreement, the Processor shall, at the
Controller's written election, either delete or return all personal data processed under
this DPA within [30 days], and delete any remaining copies within a further [30 days],
except to the extent UK law requires continued storage (e.g. suppression-list data —
see `retention-schedule.md`).

## 9. Audit

9.1 The Processor shall make available all information reasonably necessary to
demonstrate compliance with this DPA and shall allow for and contribute to audits by the
Controller or its appointed auditor, on reasonable prior written notice, no more than
[once per 12 months] absent a suspected breach.
