## How we use your details to ask for reviews

### Purpose

After you buy a product or service from us, we may contact you by [SMS and/or email] to
ask about your experience and, if you're willing, invite you to leave a review of us on
[Google / other platform]. We use this to understand how we're doing and to help other
people considering using us make an informed choice. We never ask you to leave a
positive review, and we never filter who receives this invitation based on how happy we
think you were with us.

### Our lawful basis

We rely on one of the following, recorded against your contact record at the time we
collected your details:

- **Soft opt-in** under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 —
  because we collected your details in the course of selling you a product or service,
  the review request concerns that same or a similar product/service, and we offered you
  a simple way to opt out both at that time and in every message since; or
- **Your consent**, where you told us separately that you're happy to receive a review
  request.

Our underlying UK GDPR basis for this processing is **legitimate interests** (Article
6(1)(f)) — namely, understanding customer experience and building an honest public
reputation — balanced against your right to privacy and control over unwanted messages
(see our Legitimate Interests Assessment, available on request).

### Who we share your details with

We use **[Agency Name]**, a specialist review-automation provider, to send these
messages on our behalf, using the **GoHighLevel** customer relationship management
platform (operated by GoHighLevel / LeadConnector LLC). [Agency Name] processes your data
strictly on our instructions, under a written data processing agreement, and does not
use your data for its own purposes. GoHighLevel stores data on cloud infrastructure
located in the United States, protected by [the UK International Data Transfer Addendum
and/or the UK Extension to the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework — confirm current
mechanism, see `dpa-template.md` §6].

### How long we keep this data

- Your contact details and consent-basis record: for as long as you remain a customer,
  plus [6 years] afterwards. [Client/solicitor to confirm the period appropriate to
  their sector.]
- If you opt out, we keep a record of your opt-out **indefinitely**, so we can make sure
  we never send you another review request by mistake.

Full detail is in our retention schedule, available on request.

### Your rights

You have the right to:
- **Object** to this processing at any time — simply reply STOP to any message, click
  the unsubscribe link, or contact us using the details in our main privacy notice. We
  will stop sending you review requests immediately and will not re-add you to this
  messaging.
- Access, correct, or ask us to delete your personal data (subject to our retention
  obligations above).
- Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you're
  unhappy with how we've handled your data.

These rights sit alongside, and do not replace, the rights set out in the rest of our
privacy notice.
