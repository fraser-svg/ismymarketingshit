# Launch Roadmap — Zero to £2,000/month

> Added after the packaged gate run at the operator's request; it will be audited on the
> next `/build-agency` gate run. Figures reference the package's own pricing
> (`agency/research/offer-and-pricing.md`) and compliance rules
> (`.claude/skills/build-agency/references/uk-compliance-checklist.md`).

**Target arithmetic** (from the package pricing): £2k MRR = 7 × Tier 1 (£295/mo), or
4 × Tier 2 (£595/mo), or 3 × Tier 1 + 2 × Tier 2 (£2,075/mo) — plus ~£4,750–5,250 in
one-off setup fees on the way.

## Phase 0 — Legal foundations (Week 1, ~£450 + solicitor fee)

1. **Form a Ltd company** — Companies House online, £50, ~24h. Ltd over sole trader: you
   sign DPAs as a processor and compliance credibility is the pitch.
2. **Business bank account** — Starling/Tide/Monzo (free).
3. **Pay the ICO data protection fee** — Tier 1, £52/yr (£47 DD). Required BEFORE
   processing any client customer data (GDPR-5). See
   `agency/compliance/ico-registration-note.md`.
4. **Professional indemnity + cyber insurance** — ~£15–30/mo.
5. **Solicitor review** of `agency/legal/service-agreement-template.md`,
   `agency/compliance/dpa-template.md` and `agency/compliance/privacy-notice-template.md`
   (LEGAL-1). Budget £300–750 fixed-fee; done once, reused per client.
6. **TPS/CTPS screening subscription** (~£20/mo) — required before any cold calling.

## Phase 1 — Infrastructure (Weeks 1–2, ~£100/mo)

7. **GoHighLevel Starter** ($97/mo, 14-day trial). Upgrade to Unlimited only at ~5+
   clients when white-label matters.
8. **Build the demo sub-account** per `agency/ghl/setup-guide.md`: UK long number (LC
   Phone), fields, both workflows, templates from `agency/campaigns/`. Test on yourself —
   demos should show a live compliant review request arriving on the prospect's phone.
9. **One-page website** (GHL-hosted or Carrd/Framer): positioning + tiers from
   `agency/research/offer-and-pricing.md`, compliance-first section, booking calendar,
   your own privacy notice.
10. **Own Google Business Profile + LinkedIn**; run your own review flow on early
    clients.

## Phase 2 — Sales assets (Week 2)

11. **Pitch**: most competitors sell review gating — a banned practice under the DMCC Act
    2024 (in force April 2025, CMA fines up to 10% of turnover). You deliver the same
    outcome legally, audit trail included. One-page PDF + 15-minute live demo.
12. **Pick ONE launch niche** from the package's top three (plumbers/electricians,
    salons, accountants) and stay in it until £2k MRR. Accountants: slower close, higher
    tiers. Salons: volume, Tier 1.

## Phase 3 — Outreach (Weeks 2–10) — cold outreach done legally

- **Cold calls to businesses: permitted** with CTPS **and** TPS screening (sole traders
  are individual subscribers — PECR-5), caller identification, and honouring do-not-call
  requests. Primary channel for trades and salons.
- **Cold email: corporate subscribers only** (Ltd companies, e.g. accountancy firms),
  identified sender + working opt-out in every message. **Never cold-email sole traders
  or partnerships** — they need consent. Practical rule: email accountants; call or visit
  trades and salons.
- **Unrestricted channels**: direct mail, LinkedIn, walk-ins, networking (BNI, chamber of
  commerce), referral partnerships (web designers, trade suppliers, bookkeepers).
- **Volume reality**: ~10–15 conversations per booked demo, ~3–5 demos per close from
  cold. Seven clients in ~90 days ≈ 15–20 screened dials/day plus 5–10 walk-ins or
  B2B emails, sustained.

## Phase 4 — Close and onboard (from Week 3)

13. Follow `agency/ops/onboarding-checklist.md` strictly: contract + DPA signed BEFORE
    data import (GDPR-2); client ICO fee confirmed (GDPR-5); privacy notice updated
    (GDPR-3); consent-basis audit — contacts without a recorded basis are not imported
    (PECR-1).
14. First 2 clients: founding discount on the SETUP FEE only — never on compliance
    scope — in exchange for a case study and a review of your agency.
15. With the snapshot saved, each Tier 1 build is under a day; marginal cost of client
    #5 is near zero.

## The maths

| Route | Clients | MRR | One-off setup cash |
|---|---|---|---|
| All Tier 1 (£295) | 7 | £2,065 | £5,250 |
| All Tier 2 (£595) | 4 | £2,380 | £5,000 |
| Mixed: 3×T1 + 2×T2 | 5 | £2,075 | £4,750 |

Running costs at target: ~£250–400/mo (GHL, SMS usage, insurance, ICO fee, TPS checker,
website) → ~£1,600+ gross margin at £2k MRR. Realistic pace with consistent outreach:
first client by week 4–6; £2k MRR around month 3–5; likely cash-positive from client 2
thanks to setup fees.
