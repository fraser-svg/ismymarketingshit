- agency/ops/complaint-optout-sop.md [GDPR-4] Section 1.2 Step 3 (manual suppression request) instructs staff to "Set `Opt-Out Channel` = `Phone` or `Email` (whichever they used to request opt-out)" (line 50), and the incident-log guidance in Section 4.2 lists "Channel: SMS, Email, Phone, or other" as valid values. But the `Opt-Out Channel` custom field is defined, once and authoritatively, in `agency/ghl/snapshot-spec.md` §2 as a single-select dropdown with only two permitted values: `SMS` and `Email` — confirmed identically in `agency/ghl/setup-guide.md` Step 2, Field 7 ("Options (enter both): `SMS`, `Email`"). There is no `Phone` option anywhere the field is actually built. A customer who opts out by phone therefore cannot have that channel accurately recorded in the GHL audit-trail field the rest of the pack relies on for PECR-3/GDPR-4 evidence — staff must either mis-record the channel as SMS/Email or leave it contradicting the SOP's own instruction. → Either add `Phone` (and/or `Other`) as a permitted value of the `Opt-Out Channel` custom field in `agency/ghl/snapshot-spec.md` §2 and `agency/ghl/setup-guide.md` Step 2 Field 7, or change `agency/ops/complaint-optout-sop.md` to map phone-received opt-out requests onto one of the two existing values (e.g. record channel as the value of the *messaging* channel being suppressed, with the phone-call itself logged only in the free-text incident log, not the constrained custom field) so the instructions never ask staff to enter a dropdown value the system does not offer.
