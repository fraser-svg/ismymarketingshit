## 2. Client obligations

The Client shall:

2.1 Establish and record, for each contact before any review-request message is sent to
them, the lawful basis (consent or the four-limb PECR soft opt-in) in accordance with
`agency/compliance/lawful-basis-analysis.md` and `agency/compliance/pecr-consent-flow.md`,
and maintain the `Review Consent Basis` custom field accurately in GoHighLevel.

2.2 Keep its own privacy notice up to date to reflect the review-request processing,
using the insert at `agency/compliance/privacy-notice-template.md` as a starting point,
and ensure customers are informed before their data is used for this purpose.

2.3 Register with, and pay the annual data protection fee to, the Information
Commissioner's Office (ICO), per `agency/compliance/ico-registration-note.md`, and
provide evidence of registration to the Agency on request.

2.4 Not use, and not instruct or permit any employee, contractor, or third party to use,
any side channel (personal accounts, bought-in contact lists, in-person pressure, or
otherwise) to solicit, incentivise, fabricate, or gate reviews outside the compliant
process the Agency provides — see clause 4 and
`agency/compliance/dmcc-review-policy.md`.

2.5 Provide the Agency with accurate, timely information reasonably required to
configure and operate the Services (e.g. Google Business Profile access, customer
contact data, transaction references).

2.6 Promptly notify the Agency of any complaint, regulatory enquiry (ICO or CMA), or
suspected personal data breach connected to the Services.

## 3. Agency warranties

The Agency warrants that, in providing the Services, it shall not:

3.1 Configure or operate any workflow that gates reviews — every eligible customer must
receive an identical public review invitation (DMCC-2).

3.2 Configure or suggest any incentive, discount, prize draw, or payment in exchange for
a review, disclosed or not (DMCC-1).

3.3 Draft, post, or submit any review purporting to be from a customer, or supply
pre-written review text to a customer, or use any agency-controlled account to post a
review (DMCC-5).

3.4 Configure any workflow or provide any template that offers a refund, payment, or
other benefit conditional on a customer editing or removing a review (DMCC-3).

3.5 Send, or configure the Client's account to send, any review-request message outside
the quiet hours (08:00–20:00 UK time) or beyond the capped frequency (initial request
plus a maximum of two follow-ups per transaction) set out in
`agency/ghl/workflows/review-request-sequence.md`.

## 4. What neither party will do

Both parties agree that, throughout this Agreement, neither shall engage in, direct, or
knowingly permit: fake or incentivised reviews; review gating; paying to alter or remove
reviews; or impersonating a customer in a review — as more fully described in
`agency/compliance/dmcc-review-policy.md`. A breach of this clause by either party is a
material breach entitling the other party to terminate immediately under clause 8.2.

5.2 The parties shall comply with the Data Processing Agreement at
