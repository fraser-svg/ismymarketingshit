# Offer & Pricing — UK Review-Automation Agency

## Positioning Statement

We help UK service businesses turn everyday customer contact — a job invoice, an
appointment, a salon visit, a completed tax return — into a steady, fully compliant
stream of genuine Google reviews. Built on GoHighLevel and run for the client under
their own business name, our service automates the *asking* while keeping every message
inside UK law: PECR-compliant consent, a recorded lawful basis, an opt-out that works
immediately, and a public review invitation that goes to every eligible customer, not
just the happy ones. Where most reputation agencies sell software or vague "reputation
management," we sell a done-for-you, audit-ready compliance posture as standard — the
thing nervous business owners (and their regulators) actually want, and the thing the
rest of the market treats as an afterthought.

the client's own business name and branding.

## Compliance as the Differentiator

The established players in this market — Birdeye, Trustpilot, Feefo, ReviewTrackers, and
the general run of UK reputation agencies — sell aggregation, monitoring, and *passive*
compliance (removing flagged reviews after the fact). None of them lead with PECR soft
opt-in consent capture as a core, front-loaded requirement before any message goes out,
and the CMA's active 2026 enforcement action against fake and manipulated reviews means
that gap is now a genuine business risk for clients, not just a legal nicety.

We flip that: compliance is the first thing we sell, not the small print.

- Every client onboarding starts by establishing and documenting the PECR lawful basis
  (consent or the four-limb soft opt-in) *before* a single message is configured.
- Every workflow bakes in immediate opt-out suppression, quiet-hours sending, and a
  frequency cap — so the client is never one complaint away from an ICO enquiry.
- Every review ask goes to every eligible customer, identically — a client can defend
  their review profile to a platform, a regulator, or a journalist without flinching.
- The sales pitch to a plumber, salon owner or accountant is not "we'll get you more
  reviews" (a promise a cowboy competitor can also make, cheaply and riskily) — it's
  "we'll get you more reviews *and* you'll never have to worry about a fake-review
  investigation, a PECR complaint, or an ICO fine." For regulated or reputation-sensitive
  clients (accountants, solicitors, dentists), that reassurance is the actual product.

## What We Will Never Do

The following are hard limits, not marketing choices, and apply to every tier and every
client without exception:

- **No review gating.** We never pre-screen sentiment or route unhappy customers away
  from the public review ask; every eligible customer receives the same public invitation
  (DMCC-2). Internal feedback capture, if used, is additive — it never replaces or
  suppresses the public ask.
- **No incentivised reviews.** No discounts, prize draws, or payment of any kind for
  leaving a review, disclosed or not (DMCC-1).
- **No fake reviews.** We never draft, post, or submit reviews on a customer's behalf,
  "help" customers write reviews with pre-written text, or use agency-controlled accounts
  to post reviews (DMCC-5).
- **No paying to alter or remove reviews.** We never offer refunds, payments or other
  benefits conditional on a customer editing or taking down a review. Making things right
  for a genuinely unhappy customer is part of good service; buying the review's removal
  is not (DMCC-3).

We would rather lose a prospective client who wants us to break one of these rules than
take on the legal and reputational risk of doing so — and we say this explicitly in
sales conversations, because it is itself part of the pitch.
