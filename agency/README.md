# UK-Legal GoHighLevel Review-Automation Agency Launch Package

## What This Is

This package is a complete, UK-compliant launch kit for a review-automation agency operating within the GoHighLevel platform. It covers market research, legal compliance, GHL configuration, campaign copywriting, and operational procedures — everything needed to onboard clients and run review-request workflows lawfully under GDPR, PECR, and the Defamation Act 2013 / Online Safety Act 2023.

## What This Is Not

- **No GHL API automation.** This package assumes manual workflow setup in the GHL UI, not programmatic API calls.
- **No GoHighLevel account signup included.** You must create your own GHL agency account separately before implementing these templates.
- **Not legal advice.** All legal files are templates requiring solicitor review before use (see LEGAL-1 below).

## How to Use This Package

**Recommended read order:**

1. **Research** (`agency/research/`) — Market overview, target niches, and pricing model  
   Start here to understand the opportunity and positioning.

2. **Compliance** (`agency/compliance/`) — Legal basis, consent flows, and data-handling requirements  
   Critical before touching any client data. Pay special attention to PECR (review request consent) and GDPR (data retention).

3. **GHL Setup Guide** (`agency/ghl/setup-guide.md`) — Click-by-click instructions for configuring your agency  
   Implement the template specifications in your live GHL account.

4. **Campaigns** (`agency/campaigns/`) — SMS, email, and review-response copy  
   Deploy these after GHL workflows are live.

5. **Operations** (`agency/ops/`) — Onboarding checklists, reporting, and complaint handling  
   Use these day-to-day with clients.

## Critical Limitations

**Legal files are templates, not legal advice.** You MUST have a solicitor review all compliance documents before launching or signing anything with clients. Relying on templates without legal review exposes you to regulatory action and civil liability.

**Laws change.** Verify current law against the sources listed in `agency/compliance/legal-research-notes.md` before launch and at least annually thereafter. The Data Protection Act, PECR, and advertising standards evolve; what is compliant today may not be tomorrow.
